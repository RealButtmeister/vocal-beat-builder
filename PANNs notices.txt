PANNs instrument-recognition notices

Architecture: MobileNetV1, adapted for inference from Qiuqiang Kong et al.
https://github.com/qiuqiangkong/audioset_tagging_cnn
Architecture changes: unused training augmentation, initialization and dropout
removed. Parameter names and inference layers retain the original structure.

Pretrained weights: MobileNetV1_mAP=0.389.pth, the authors' official release
https://zenodo.org/records/3987831
Creators: Qiuqiang Kong, Yin Cao, Turab Iqbal, Yuxuan Wang, Wenwu Wang,
and Mark D. Plumbley. PANNs: Large-Scale Pretrained Audio Neural Networks
for Audio Pattern Recognition (2020).
Weights license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
Verified against the official Zenodo record's metadata license, cc-by-4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The weights are downloaded unchanged and checked against the published MD5:
a419303e1c88aa1b9d2ac3811563d371

Class-label CSV: copied unchanged from the authors' repository. The AudioSet
ontology is described at https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/index.html
and released under CC BY-SA 4.0. Google AudioSet attribution applies.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Dependency: torchlibrosa 0.1.0 by Qiuqiang Kong (MIT).
https://github.com/qiuqiangkong/torchlibrosa
Installed package notices are retained in the private runtime.

The recognition outputs are model estimates from audio, not exact plugin names
or transcribed musical notes. A model score is not calibrated certainty.
